Evaluate current innovation only, using `final_packet.txt` and `final_manifest.json` together with this file. No previous assessment is part of this packet. Do not read other assessment files. Do not infer a desired score from author identities, a journal, or an earlier version. The full mathematical and numerical evidence is provided as plain text for a single fixed batch.

The model has independent stationary bounded reward streams, deterministic exogenous permanent arrivals, one selected observation per physical round, and no means, best-arm identities, future schedule, or horizon supplied to the main policy. The continuing task is reliable reporting of the complete relative epsilon-good set at every post-observation time. Area counts unresolved arm-time only outside a boundary band of width eta. The main protocol combines expected pseudo-regret with a joint high-probability label-and-area guarantee. It differs from a terminal recommendation, individual error control, and a sequence of imposed hard deadlines.

Other material supports the paper but is not asserted to be a separate main innovation: standard KL and confidence tools; an occupation-allocation lower relaxation without general causal attainability; an eventual finite-arm consistency construction using revisable intervals, strict final boundary separation, and continued exploration. The fixed-precision implemented policy does not inherit that consistency theorem.

Completed numerical evidence: four frozen eight-arm Bernoulli environments, nine methods, 160 paired replications per environment and 20,000 physical pulls per replication, giving 5,760 runs. Potential rewards are shared by arm and lifetime sample number. Benchmarks include full-history UCB with matched reporting, an anytime Growing-MOSS adaptation, a retained-history ALL-epsilon confidence adaptation (explicitly not an implementation of FAREAST or ST2), short-period admission with confidence retirement, and checkpoint allocation variants. A 20,000-step source-class parity check compares every action, service indicator, count, and C/D/V label.

The experiments include negative findings. Fine checkpoints with rho=.04 require total quotas exceeding the horizon and leave many arms unresolved. Coarser rho=.12 checkpoints remain label-reliable but do not guarantee eta=.10 completeness. A strong retained-history confidence adaptation dominates coarse FIFO in both regret and area in the stable and burst settings. Growing-MOSS has the lowest regret but substantially larger unresolved area. No run had a label error, but finite simulations do not prove uniform all-environment reliability. The tested environments are not the separated theorem family and do not verify its asymptotic rate. These results support the interpretation of joint costs, not universal algorithmic superiority.
