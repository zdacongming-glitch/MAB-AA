This supplement uses the deterministic arrival model and notation of the main article unless a result explicitly introduces a different experiment. All main risk bounds concern expected pseudo-regret at deterministic endpoints. Constants are universal unless a dependence is stated. The proofs use the original independent potential reward streams; they do not condition on realized old observations and then regard those observations as fresh.
